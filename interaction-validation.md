# Proof Instrument Interaction Validation

The interaction layer covers the shared Evidence Workspace rather than adding isolated motion per page. Carbon evidence panels, Signal Room board cards, decision and receipt panels, evidence lanes, score cells, comparison rows, counter-thesis items, receipt prerequisites, navigation links, and header controls now share restrained transform and emphasis behavior.

On fine-pointer desktop devices, evidence surfaces lift by four pixels, receive a low-opacity vermilion material wash, and sharpen their boundary. Carbon proof-loop surfaces use a corresponding Acid Chartreuse boundary. Action controls lift by three pixels, translate their directional icon, and acknowledge press with a 0.97 scale. Keyboard focus uses a visible Acid Chartreuse outline.

Fresh 390px mobile captures for `/`, `/signal`, `/market`, and `/proof` show the interaction CSS does not disrupt compact composition. The shared stylesheet applies hover-only motion inside `@media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)` and retains the project-wide `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` override, so touch layouts do not receive accidental hover behavior and users who reduce motion do not see nonessential transitions.
