# Proofcast Product Redesign Brief

## The repositioning

Proofcast is not presented as another market dashboard. It is the **proof layer for prediction**: a product that turns a market observation into a time-stamped position of belief and, eventually, a record that can be inspected after resolution. The judge should understand this in the first ten seconds without reading a feature list.

## Journey architecture

| Route | Job | Judge takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Product landing and proof-loop story | “This product has a clear, differentiated thesis.” |
| `/signal` | Live command workspace | “It is real: live market context is being handled carefully.” |
| `/market` | Evidence and forecast decision surface | “It separates market, model, and human judgement.” |
| `/proof` | Receipt and accountability surface | “It has a credible path from signal to audit trail.” |

## Visual direction: Proof Instrument

The new direction is an **industrial editorial instrument**, not a cosmic dashboard. The palette uses warm titanium (`#E8E6DE`) as the main presentation field, Carbon (`#151515`) for authority and contrast, Signal Vermilion (`#F04B2F`) for critical attention and commitment, and Acid Chartreuse (`#C8F06A`) for verified/live signal. This creates a confident, editorially sharp product presence without drifting into generic blue, soft lilac, or casino imagery.

The landing page uses oversized editorial type, a single signature proof-loop visualization, asymmetric evidence blocks, hard rules, precise data labels, and deliberate red/green semantic contrast. The workspace is darker and denser, but it is entered through the landing story rather than being mistaken for the full product identity.

## Landing narrative

1. **Hero:** “Predictions are cheap. Proof is not.” The visual shows a market signal passing through a committed forecast into a receipt.
2. **Thesis:** “A prediction market shows a price. Proofcast shows the thinking that met it.”
3. **Proof loop:** Observe → Commit → Resolve, with a concrete boundary between market, model, and personal judgement.
4. **Live credibility:** A compact live-data preview makes the DreamDEX connection visible without faking execution.
5. **Judge close:** “Open the Signal Room” leads to the live workspace.

## Non-negotiable design rules

- No generic SaaS gradient hero, glass-card swarm, or blue exchange aesthetic.
- The landing page must look intentional even before live data loads.
- The workspace may be information-dense, but every state carries a visible source and boundary.
- Live market data remains read-only; no trade, wallet, balance, or result is fabricated.
